Use the [main SmartSell README](../../README.md), [current methodology](../METHODOLOGY.md) and [publication guide](../PUBLICATION_GUIDE.md). The original archive did not supply the `audit_raw.py` mentioned by its old README; that historical reference has not been invented or silently replaced. Dataset originals remain under `data/raw/`; recorded byte-preservation checks used the original local ZIP. The redundant development archive `sell-time-estimation-aisd-main.zip` is intentionally excluded from the 130-file public repository and is not required to run or reproduce the final application.

Academic branding here is intentional archival context. The public project, app and submission documents are SmartSell Rwanda for NISR Track 1. No previous exploratory result supersedes the current chronological benchmark or its disclosed limitations.
