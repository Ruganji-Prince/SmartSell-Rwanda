The supplied preparatory webinar names these five dimensions and mentions national priorities including NST2/Vision 2050. This project ties its concrete farm-market and post-harvest problem to that framing without inventing specific policy targets or policy findings. Original academic material is retained in `docs/legacy_aisd/` with accurate attribution.

The final Advisor explanation adds economic evidence without changing historical results: `src/decision_engine.py` derives break-even RWF/kg and required percentage uplift from retained quantity and whole-lot waiting costs. A farmer can see why a higher quote may still be insufficient. Editable illustrative loss scenarios show the post-harvest retention tradeoff, with official NISR vegetable-sector context as motivation rather than daily calibration. `tests/test_break_even.py` verifies thresholds, units, monotonicity and zero-retention handling. This strengthens problem relevance, transparent methodology, usable interpretation and decision-support innovation. It does **not** establish measured farmer impact: the same 20-window, 8.03%-coverage simulation remains all SELL NOW with zero simulated gain.
