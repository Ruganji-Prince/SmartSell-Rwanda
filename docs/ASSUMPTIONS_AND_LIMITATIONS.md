# Assumptions and limitations

- Prices are historical farm-gate quotes, not guaranteed buyer offers or a live feed. Latest observation is 14 October 2026. Price gaps, collection delay, and shared source behavior may overstate short-horizon predictability.
- Two crops and observed markets only. Sparse histories can be shown in Market Trends even when advice is withheld. No unseen-market generalization is claimed.
- Raw SAS microdata are unavailable. Official vegetable-group indicators cannot establish Tomato/Carrot-specific production, loss, storage facilities, or loss duration. Zero reported stored share does not mean vegetables never enter storage.
- Published national production-use shares are not district loss estimates. Seasonal post-harvest shares are not daily deterioration rates and do not validate the simulation.
- Scenario rates, costs, and quantity are editable assumptions. The model omits changing grades, crop-specific shelf life, buyer availability, liquidity needs, temperature, humidity, and actual storage duration evidence. Do not treat a recommendation as guaranteed profit.
- Persistence may be the strongest forecast on this dataset. With flat forecasts, nonnegative loss and waiting costs mechanically favor selling now. This is an honest limitation of the available evidence, not proof that waiting never helps.
- The same-day availability assumption is unverified. Prior academic exploration reduces independence of historical test evidence. New future observations are needed for prospective confirmation.
- Price interval bands are empirical validation-error summaries with no coverage guarantee; discrete repeated prices may produce zero-width bands. Model selection and band estimation share validation data, which can make uncertainty optimistic.
- Simulated decisions require complete future windows. Coverage and selection bias are reported; these windows may not represent all farmers, markets, or missing reporting days. No realized income improvement is claimed.
- National priority alignment describes the problem's relevance to agricultural market linkages and post-harvest management, not government endorsement or a measured productivity increase.
- No NISR partnership, policy endorsement, or open-source license is asserted. Source-data rights and competition IP terms remain applicable.

Use this prototype to compare assumptions and understand tradeoffs. Check the actual market offer, crop condition, cash needs, and storage feasibility with local expertise before acting. English text has been structured for later reviewed localization; no unvalidated translations are supplied.
